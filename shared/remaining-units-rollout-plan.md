## Per-Unit Quality Gate

- Every lesson must use the mixed teaching flow:
  - intuition first,
  - concise formal chunks,
  - quick checks,
  - worked examples,
  - practice sets,
  - common mistakes,
  - summary and next-lesson bridge.
- Every lesson must fully teach the concept without assuming unstated prerequisite steps.
- Every lesson must include explicit assumptions and at least one misconception correction.
- Unit cannot be marked complete until lint/tests pass and links resolve in `/learn/library`.

## Delivery Cadence

- Complete one unit at a time.
- Run lint/tests after each unit.
- Publish progress note in changelog after each completed unit.
